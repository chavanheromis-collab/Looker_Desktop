📄 Print to PDF Instructions
When the print dialog opens:
  1. Destination: Select "Save as PDF"
  2. Layout: Select "Landscape"
  3. Paper size: Select "A4" or "Letter"
  4. Margins: Select "Default" or "Minimum"
  5. Scale: Keep at "Fit to page" or adjust to 100%
  6. Click "Save" to download your PDF
